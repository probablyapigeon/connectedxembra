CORE METHODOLOGICAL RESULT
==========================

Predictive dependence on history is NOT sufficient evidence
of intrinsic path dependence.

A lossy representation of the present can make historical
information predictive because history acts as a proxy for
omitted present-state information.

A stronger path-dependence test therefore requires matching
systems on a causally sufficient present-state representation
before testing whether differing histories produce differing
future evolution under identical future inputs.
